their inbox · welcome email from the campaign step 5 · the first email
From: Trained, not supported <hello@trainednotsupported.org> Subject: You're one of us now.

Lauren,

A few hours ago you added your name to a petition. Maybe it took thirty seconds, maybe you read every word. Either way, you said something out loud that thousands of others have been saying quietly.

We're not going to spam you. We're going to do three things, and we wanted to tell you upfront what they are:

We'll keep you in the loop. Once a month, a short note from us — how many more have signed, what employers are doing in response, what the wider network is talking about. Nothing more.

We'll ask for your story, once. If you want to share what it's actually like being a trained supporter at your company — anonymously or not — we'll send you a short prompt. The stories are how this stops being a number and starts being a movement.

We'll share things that might help. Not sales emails. Things from across the peer-support world that we think might be useful — events, free resources, tools other supporters have found valuable. You can ignore them or use them.

If at any point this stops feeling worth it, one click and you're out. No hard feelings.

For now — thank you for showing up. You're one of us now.

— The Trained, not supported team

From: Trained, not supported <hello@trainednotsupported.org> Subject: A few things that might actually help

Lauren,

Since you signed, 612 more supporters have added their names. That's 612 more people refusing to do this work in silence.

A lot of you have been asking the same question: what can I actually do, while I wait for my employer to take this seriously?

Three things we'd point you to, all free:

Find out where your network really stands. A 10-minute risk assessment built around the ISO 45003 standard for psychological health at work. It gives you a personal score and a forwardable PDF for your HR lead. Some signatories have used it to start conversations they couldn't start before.

A free hour of training on a structured approach for difficult conversations — what to do when a colleague comes to you, and how to look after yourself afterward. Built by Everymind at Work, who run a lot of the support infrastructure for big UK employers. Free for any signatory.

Come to a roundtable. Small, virtual, 25-30 supporters at a time. No selling, just MHFAs talking honestly with other MHFAs about what's working and what isn't. Next one is the 18th.

Use what's useful. Ignore what isn't.

— The team
